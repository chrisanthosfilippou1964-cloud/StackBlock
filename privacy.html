Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

About this game

Stack is a free browser game. This policy explains what information the game and its advertising partners may handle when you play.

What is stored on your device

The game saves a few things in your browser’s local storage so they are there next time: your best score, your recent runs, your ability charges, your sound setting, and whether you have already seen the tutorial. This information stays on your device. We do not receive it, and you can remove it at any time by clearing this site’s data in your browser settings.

Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not ask for your name, email address or any other personal details to play.

Advertising and cookies

This site uses Google AdSense to show ads. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your previous visits to this and other websites. Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and other sites on the internet.

Where the law requires it, a consent message is shown so you can accept or refuse the use of cookies for ads. You can also opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google’s Ads Settings. To learn how Google uses data from sites that use its services, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Children

This game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your choices

  • Clear this site’s data in your browser to delete saved scores and settings.
  • Use Google’s Ads Settings to control personalised ads.
  • Use your browser’s settings to block or delete cookies.
